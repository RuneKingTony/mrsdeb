# Surface brief: Amare Kharis marketing site (all routes)

Scope: `/`, `/about`, `/businesses`, `/services/:id`, `/contacts`, plus shared Navbar and Footer. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: C-suite executives and HNIs. Action: start a conversation (WhatsApp first, then phone/email). Proof: real service copy and photography only.
Constraints: palette pinned (#1b1b1b, #2b2b2b, gold #ca8a04, warm white/grey). No backend; contact composes mailto. No skill percentages, no dead social links, no newsletter.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site as a fashion house's digital flagship, rendered in black and gold: monumental serif type, vast dark negative space, slow full-bleed photography. It refuses the concierge default of a hero photo with overlay text, then icon-card grids, then a form.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #1b1b1b ground, #2b2b2b raised fields, gold #ca8a04 used rarely (primary action, active marks, hairline accents), bone #f4efe4 text, stone #a8a29e secondary. Bodoni Moda for display, Hanken Grotesk tracked caps for labels and body, Ballet "A" monogram. Hairline 1px rules, square corners, understated type controls in place of chips.

STORY: The visitor understands at once that one trusted executive handles personal and business affairs, comes to believe it through calm and precise presentation, and opens WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Hairline nav on top. Left 55%: "Amare Kharis" set across two lines, about 9vw, bottom-aligned, a one-line offer below it, a solid gold "Message on WhatsApp" button and an underlined "Explore the businesses" link. Right 45%: a tall photo window that crossfades slowly through three looks (assistance, travel, representation), each with a small caption and link, plus a pause control.

FORM: luxury-fashion-flagship (challenger, chosen by user), position: competitive alternate; seed key 352e5127. Signature interaction: the slow look-to-look crossfade with captions. Businesses read as a typographic index whose rows reverse to gold on hover or focus.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
