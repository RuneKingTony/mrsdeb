# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Busy C-suite executives and high-net-worth individuals (HNIs), mostly in Nigeria and travelling abroad, who need a trusted business executive / personal assistant or concierge to handle their personal affairs, business representation, travel and projects. Corporates buying gifting, souvenirs and project organization are a secondary audience.

## Product Purpose
Marketing site for Amare Kharis Services. It presents the business lines and professional services and gets the visitor to make an enquiry by WhatsApp, phone or email. Success means a qualified visitor reaches out.

## Positioning
A single trusted, discreet executive who manages a principal's personal and business affairs end to end: travel, representation, projects, households, gifting. One relationship instead of many vendors.

## Operating Context
Visitors arrive from Instagram, WhatsApp shares and referrals, often on a phone. Enquiries happen over WhatsApp (+234 703 490 7189), phone (+234 806 127 8922) and email (amare.kharis@gmail.com, contact@amarekharis.com).

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Create React App + Tailwind 3 + framer-motion + react-router. Routes: `/`, `/about`, `/businesses`, `/services/:id`, `/contacts`.
- No backend. Contact form composes a `mailto:` message; WhatsApp is a direct `wa.me` link. No newsletter.
- Seven business lines with full copy live in `src/serviceContext.js`.

## Brand Commitments
- Name: Amare Kharis (wordmark uses a Ballet script "A").
- Colour scheme is binding: near-black #1b1b1b / #2b2b2b surfaces, gold (Tailwind yellow-600, #ca8a04) accent, white and warm grey text.
- Tagline: "Perfection and excellence with style."

## Evidence on Hand
Photography in `src/assets/` (black.jpeg, bout.jpeg, jnm.jpeg, skillss.jpeg, image1/2, window). Only social presence confirmed: Instagram. No testimonials, client logos, or metrics exist; do not fabricate them, and do not present skill percentages.

## Product Principles
1. Discretion over spectacle: the client is buying trust and confidentiality.
2. Every page leads to a direct, human channel (WhatsApp first).
3. Real content only: no invented proof.
